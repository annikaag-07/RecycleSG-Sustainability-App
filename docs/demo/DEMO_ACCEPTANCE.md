## Scope and handoff rules

- Repository: `softwarelab3/2006-SCS1-X2`; implementation under `Lab3/`.
- Working branch: `nick/recyclesg-integration`. Preserve unrelated changes; do not push or reset a shared database without permission.
- Recommended presentation: laptop browser, mobile-sized viewport, pre-created confirmed account, prepared plastic-bottle image. Phone presentation requires its own rehearsal.
- Story: find somewhere to recycle → record a drop → receive recognition → see personal impact → contribute toward a community milestone.
- Keep deletion, registration/email delivery, password recovery, moderation, milestone approval, social posts/comments/likes and notification delivery out of scope. Missing endpoints for these are acceptable.
- Do not describe material recognition as proof that an item was physically recycled. Explain the prototype's acceptance rule.
- CO₂ is an illustrative estimate (currently 0.1 kg/item), not a measured outcome. Eco Point contributions do not establish that money was donated or an environmental project completed.
- All checklist boxes start unchecked unless supported by recorded evidence. Use `PASS`, `FAIL`, `BLOCKED`, or `NOT RUN`; do not turn blocked checks into passes.
